Harmony Draught Calculator

Variants:

⚠ Warning

  • Do NOT use the entire stack of ingredients at the Alchemy Table. It will consume the entire stack and give you only 1 single elixir.

  • Only use the exact amount required to craft 1 elixir.

  • Example: Elixir of Fury ×1
    Dwarf Mushroom ×4 / Ash Sap ×1 / Lion Blood ×4 / Purified Water ×3

Notes

  • You may end up getting more elixirs depending on your Alchemy Mastery.

  • All shown required elixirs are green-grade elixirs.

  • Blue-grade elixirs can be used as a substitute to green-grade elixirs at a 1:3 ratio.
    30 green elixirs = 10 blue elixirs.

  • Blue/Green-grade mushrooms can be used as a substitute to normal-grade mushrooms at a 8/4:1 ration.
    80 white mushrooms = 2 green/1 blue mushroom.

  • To craft draughts use processing/simple alchemy.
    To craft elixirs use alchemy table at your residence.
